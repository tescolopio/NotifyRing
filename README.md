# NotifyRing
Many older adults miss critical text messages, pharmacy updates, doctor portal alerts, or family chats because passive notification banners are easy to overlook, hard to read, or gone before they reach their phone. NotiftyRing bridges that gap by transforming selected app notifications into familiar, full-screen incoming phone calls. 

> **Initial scaffold only:** Setup, app selection, speech, call controls, persistence,
> and alert history are not implemented yet. Do not rely on this app for care or
> time-sensitive alerts. The call and notification integration has not yet been
> validated on a device.
